# Duluth HQ Office Guide

Our headquarters is the **Burrow**, a four-story building at 1200 Lakeside Avenue in Duluth, Minnesota. We moved here in 2025.

## Floor by Floor

- **Floor 1:** Front desk, badge pickup, and the Superposition Cafe.
- **Floor 2:** Engineering, IT, and the Tech Pantry (IT help desk).
- **Floor 3:** Marketing, People team, and the nap pods.
- **Floor 4:** Flavor Science lab. Access requires a red badge. No outside food allowed (ironic, we know).

## The Superposition Cafe

Breakfast is free from 7:30am to 9:30am. Lunch is subsidized: $4 for any meal. The cafe also has the **Snack Wall**, where every QQS product is free, all day, every day.

## Nap Pods

There are 6 nap pods on Floor 3. Book them in SnackStack in 20-minute blocks. Maximum two blocks per day.

## Wi-Fi

Employees connect to **QuokkaNet** using their SnackStack login. Guests use **QuokkaNet-Guest**; the front desk prints a new guest password every morning.

## Parking

Free parking is available in the Lakeside Avenue garage. Bike storage is in the basement. Employees who bike to work at least 3 days a week get an extra free snack box each month.

## Biscuit

Biscuit, our golden retriever, is in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Please do not feed Biscuit Heisenberg Jerky. He has already had enough.
