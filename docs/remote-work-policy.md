# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

Quokka Quantum Snacks is a hybrid company. We believe snacks are best invented together, but laundry is best done at home.

## Core Crunch Days

All office-based employees are expected in the office on **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**. We call these **Core Crunch Days**. Monday and Friday are flexible: work from home or from the office, your choice.

Factory Operations staff are on a shift schedule and are not covered by this policy.

## Home Office Stipend

New hybrid employees receive a one-time **$650 home office stipend** for things like a desk, chair, or monitor. We also reimburse up to **$40 per month** for home internet. Submit receipts in SnackStack.

## Work From Anywhere

Each employee may work fully remotely for up to **4 weeks per year** from anywhere, including outside the United States, as long as:

- You are reachable during your team's core hours (10am to 3pm Central Time).
- Your manager approves at least 30 days in advance.
- You are not travelling during Launch Week (the second week of March).

## Equipment

Company laptops may be taken home. Monitors and the office espresso machine may not. (We are still looking for the person who took the espresso machine in 2024.)
