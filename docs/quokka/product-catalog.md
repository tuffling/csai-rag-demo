# Product Catalog (Fall 2026)

All Quokka Quantum Snacks products are made at our Duluth, Minnesota factory.

## Superposition Puffs - $3.49

Our best-seller. Corn puffs that are both spicy and mild until you open the bag. Available in Habanero/Honey and Sriracha/Sour Cream. Launched 2018.

## Entangled Pretzels - $4.99

Pretzels sold in linked pairs. When you eat one, the other one gets saltier. (It doesn't, but customers swear it does.) Launched 2019.

## Schrodinger's Cheddar - $3.99

A mystery-flavor cheese cracker. The flavor is printed on the inside of the bag, so you don't know until you open it. Current flavors in rotation: Smoked Gouda, Jalapeno Jack, Maple Cheddar, and Truffle Parmesan.

## Heisenberg Jerky - $7.99

Plant-based mushroom jerky. You can know the flavor or the texture, but never both. Launched 2023. Our fastest-growing product.

## Qubit Bites - $2.49

Bite-sized chocolate-covered rice crisps sold in packs of 8. Launched 2024. Popular with kids and graduate students.

## Discontinued Products

- **Wave Function Wafers** (2018-2022): discontinued after the 2022 recall. See the incident postmortem.
- **Dark Matter Mints** (2020-2021): discontinued because customers said they "couldn't find them."
