# Travel & Expense Policy

## Booking Travel

All business travel is booked through SnackStack. Book at least 14 days in advance when possible.

- **Flights under 6 hours:** economy class.
- **Flights 6 hours or longer:** premium economy.
- **Hotels:** up to $220 per night in most cities, up to $320 per night in New York, San Francisco, Toronto, and London.

## Per Diem (Meals)

Instead of submitting meal receipts, travelers receive a daily meal allowance (per diem):

- **$75 per day** for domestic travel (within the United States).
- **$110 per day** for international travel, including Canada.

## Receipts & Reimbursement

Receipts are required for any single expense over **$25**. Submit all expenses in SnackStack within **30 days** of returning from your trip. Expenses submitted after 30 days require approval from the CFO.

Reimbursements are paid with your next paycheck.

## Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client dinners that were approved in advance by a VP.

## Bringing Snacks

Employees traveling to client meetings may take up to 3 boxes of QQS products from the Snack Wall as gifts. Please don't check an entire suitcase of Superposition Puffs. (Again.)
