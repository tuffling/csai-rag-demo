# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to the Burrow! Here's what your first few weeks look like.

## Day 1

- [ ] Pick up your badge at the front desk on Floor 1. Bring a photo ID.
- [ ] Collect your laptop from the Tech Pantry (Floor 2).
- [ ] Install QuokkaAuth and log into SnackStack.
- [ ] Meet your **Snack Buddy**, an experienced Quokka assigned to answer your questions during your first 90 days.
- [ ] Take your first official photo with Biscuit (if it's a Tuesday or Thursday).

## Week 1

- [ ] Complete **Food Safety 101** in SnackStack. Everyone must finish it within their first **14 days**, including software engineers.
- [ ] Attend the **First Friday Taste Panel**, where new hires rate the next season's flavor candidates.
- [ ] Set up your home office if you are hybrid (see the Remote Work Policy for the stipend).

## First 30 Days

- [ ] Have a 1:1 with your manager to set 90-day goals.
- [ ] Attend one "Snack & Ask" session with the CEO.
- [ ] Tour the Duluth factory (tours run every Wednesday at 10am; hairnets provided).

## First 90 Days

- [ ] Complete your 90-day check-in with your manager and your Snack Buddy.
- [ ] Become eligible to submit your own flavor idea to the quarterly **Flavor Pitch Night**. Winning ideas get a $1,000 bonus and their name on the bag.
