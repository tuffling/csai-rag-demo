# Time Off Policy

This document describes how vacation and time off work at Quokka Quantum Snacks.

## Vacation Days

Full-time employees receive **15 days of paid time off** per year. Vacation days are granted all at once on January 1st.

Unused vacation days **do not roll over**. Any days not used by December 31st are lost.

## Requesting Time Off

Email your manager at least one week before your planned time off. Your manager will forward approved requests to HR.

## Sick Days

Sick days are counted as part of your 15 PTO days. There is no separate sick leave.

## Birthdays

Employees may leave two hours early on their birthday.
