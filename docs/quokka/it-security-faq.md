# IT & Security FAQ

## Where do I get IT help?

Visit the **Tech Pantry** on Floor 2 of the Burrow (open 8am to 6pm), call extension **4242**, or post in the #tech-pantry chat channel.

## How often do I need to change my password?

Every **180 days**. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases are encouraged, for example "purple-quokka-eats-jerky".

## What is QuokkaAuth?

QuokkaAuth is our two-factor authentication app. It is required for SnackStack, email, and anything that connects to company systems. Install it on your phone during your first day.

## I lost my laptop. What do I do?

Call extension 4242 **immediately**, even outside business hours (the after-hours line forwards to the on-call engineer). IT can remotely lock and wipe the laptop. You will not get in trouble for losing a laptop, but you will get in trouble for not reporting it.

## How do I report a phishing email?

Use the "Report Phish" button in your email client, or forward the email to security@qqsnacks.example. Common phishing attempts at QQS pretend to be from "Priya" asking you to buy gift cards. Priya will never ask you to buy gift cards.

## Can I use personal AI tools at work?

Only tools on the approved list in SnackStack. **Never paste recipes, flavor formulas, or anything about Project Tachyon into an external AI tool.**

## What laptop will I get?

Everyone gets a 14-inch laptop. Engineering and Flavor Science staff may request a 16-inch model.
