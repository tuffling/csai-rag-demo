# Company History

## Timeline

- **2016:** Priya Halvorsen-Mbeki invents Superposition Puffs in her apartment kitchen.
- **2017:** Quokka Quantum Snacks is founded. First sales at the Duluth farmers market. Revenue in year one: $38,000.
- **2018:** Superposition Puffs and Wave Function Wafers enter regional grocery stores in Minnesota and Wisconsin.
- **2019:** First factory opens in Duluth. Entangled Pretzels launch.
- **2020:** Dark Matter Mints launch (and quietly disappear a year later).
- **2021:** Series B funding round of **$48 million**, led by **Greylake Ventures**. Company grows to 140 employees.
- **2022:** The Great Wafer Recall. Wave Function Wafers are discontinued.
- **2023:** Heisenberg Jerky launches. QQS reaches 250 employees.
- **2024:** Expansion into Canada. Qubit Bites launch.
- **2025:** Move to the new headquarters, the Burrow. New Employee Handbook replaces older policies.
- **2026:** Announcement of **Project Tachyon**, a secret new product line. Details are confidential, but it is rumored to involve frozen desserts.

## Mission

"Make snacks so good they exist in two states at once: delicious and gone."

## Mascot

Quincy the Quokka, based on CEO Priya's plush toy. Quincy appears on every bag and smiles because quokkas always look like they're smiling.
