# New Sauce: Diablo Supernova

Taco Bell is adding a new sauce packet, **Diablo Supernova**, to its lineup in **January 2027**.

## How hot is it?

Diablo Supernova is the **hottest sauce Taco Bell has ever sold**, hotter than Diablo. It's made with **ghost peppers and mango**, so it starts sweet and then gets very, very hot.

## Packet messages

Like the other sauce packets, each Diablo Supernova packet has a short message printed on it. The 2027 packets have **space-themed messages**, such as "Houston, we have a flavor problem" and "One small bite for man."

## Where to get it

Diablo Supernova packets will be free with any order at participating U.S. locations. Bottles of Diablo Supernova will also be sold in grocery stores starting in **spring 2027**.
