# Taco Bell Announces the Crunchwrap Galaxy

**October 2026:** Taco Bell's newest limited-time item, the **Crunchwrap Galaxy**, arrives at participating U.S. locations on **November 12, 2026** for **$5.99**.

## What's in it

The Crunchwrap Galaxy is built on a **blue-corn "nebula" tortilla** and filled with seasoned beef, warm nacho cheese sauce, a crunchy tostada shell dusted with **"stardust" chili-lime seasoning**, lettuce, tomatoes and cool ranch sour cream. A vegetarian version swaps the beef for black beans.

## Price and availability

- **Price:** $5.99 on its own, or $8.99 as a combo with a drink and Nacho Fries.
- Combos come with a **glow-in-the-dark souvenir cup** while supplies last.
- Available until **January 31, 2027**, or while supplies last.
- About 610 calories.

## Early access

Members of the new Fourth Meal Club can order the Crunchwrap Galaxy **48 hours early**, starting November 10, 2026.
