# Introducing the Fourth Meal Club

Taco Bell is launching the **Fourth Meal Club**, a late-night membership, on **December 1, 2026**.

## How it works

- **Cost:** $4.99 per month, billed through the Taco Bell app.
- Members get **one free Late Night Taco every day** between **10 p.m. and 4 a.m.**
- Members get **48-hour early access** to every new menu item.
- Members get a **secret "Moon Menu"** in the app with three items that aren't sold to anyone else.
- Cancel anytime in the app.

## The Moon Menu

The first three Moon Menu items are the **Midnight Melt Quesadilla**, **Cinnamon Comet Twists** and the **Eclipse Freeze** (a black-cherry frozen drink).

## Limits

One free taco per member per day. The free taco can't be combined with other offers. The Fourth Meal Club is only available in the United States.
