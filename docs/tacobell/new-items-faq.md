# FAQ: What's New at Taco Bell (Winter 2026-27)

**Is the Crunchwrap Galaxy vegetarian?**
There's a vegetarian version that uses black beans instead of seasoned beef. Just ask for the "veggie Galaxy."

**Do I need the Fourth Meal Club to get the Crunchwrap Galaxy?**
No. Anyone can order it starting November 12, 2026. Fourth Meal Club members can order it two days early.

**Can I get my free Fourth Meal Club taco at lunch?**
No. The free daily taco is only available between 10 p.m. and 4 a.m.

**Is Cantina Orbit opening anywhere besides Las Vegas?**
Not yet. Las Vegas is the only Cantina Orbit location announced so far.

**Is Diablo Supernova replacing Diablo sauce?**
No. Diablo stays on the menu. Diablo Supernova is an extra option for people who want something even hotter.

**Where can I see the Moon Menu?**
Only Fourth Meal Club members can see it, in the "Moon Menu" tab of the Taco Bell app.
