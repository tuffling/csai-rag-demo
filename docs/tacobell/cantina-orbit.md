# Taco Bell Cantina Orbit: A Restaurant That Spins

Taco Bell's newest restaurant concept, **Cantina Orbit**, opens in **Las Vegas, Nevada** in **March 2027**.

## What makes it different

- The dining room sits on a **rotating floor** that makes one full turn every **45 minutes**, so every seat gets a view of the Strip.
- Orders are placed on tablets at each table, and food arrives on a **conveyor ring** around the edge of the room.
- A "sauce bar" has **12 hot sauces**, including two that are only sold at Cantina Orbit.
- It's open **24 hours a day**.

## Exclusive items

Cantina Orbit is the only place to get the **Orbit Platter** (a sampler of six mini tacos, $14.99) and the **Zero-Gravity Churro Sundae** ($6.49).

## Reservations

Walk-ins are welcome, but tables on the rotating floor can be reserved up to **30 days in advance** in the Taco Bell app.
