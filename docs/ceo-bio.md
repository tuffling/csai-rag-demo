# About Our Founder & CEO: Priya Halvorsen-Mbeki

Priya Halvorsen-Mbeki founded Quokka Quantum Snacks in 2017 and has been CEO ever since.

## Background

Priya grew up in Thunder Bay, Ontario, and moved to Minnesota for college. She was a physics PhD student at the University of Minnesota studying quantum optics before leaving the program in 2016 to start a snack company. She says the decision came to her "while staring at a vending machine at 3am in the lab."

## The First Snack

The first QQS product, Superposition Puffs, was invented by accident in Priya's apartment kitchen in Duluth. She was trying to make a spicy seasoning and a honey glaze at the same time and spilled one into the other. Her roommate tasted the result and said, "It's spicy but also... not?" The name followed.

## Leadership Style

Priya holds an open "Snack & Ask" session every other Friday at 4pm in the Superposition Cafe, where any employee can ask her anything.
