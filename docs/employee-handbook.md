# Quokka Quantum Snacks - Employee Handbook (2025 Edition)

Welcome to Quokka Quantum Snacks (QQS)! This handbook covers the policies every Quokka needs to know. It applies to all full-time employees starting January 1, 2025.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive **23 days of paid time off** per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly (just under 2 days per month), so you can't use your whole year on January 2nd.

In addition to regular PTO, every employee gets their birthday off. We call this **Quokka Day**. If your birthday falls on a weekend, take the Friday before or the Monday after.

Up to **5 unused PTO days roll over** into the next year. Anything above 5 days disappears on December 31st, so plan accordingly.

PTO requests must be submitted in **SnackStack** (our internal HR portal) at least **two weeks in advance** for anything longer than two days. Shorter requests just need your manager's approval in SnackStack.

If a PTO request is denied and you believe the decision was unfair, you can escalate it to the **VP of People**.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Every employee gets **10 paid sick days** per year. Sick days do not roll over. You do not need a doctor's note for absences of three days or fewer.

## Blackout Period: Launch Week

Every year during **Launch Week** (the second week of March), when we release our new spring flavors, PTO is not approved for the Flavor Science, Marketing, and Factory Operations teams except in emergencies.

## Company Holidays

QQS observes 11 company holidays, plus a company-wide shutdown between December 24 and January 1. The full list is published in SnackStack every November.

## Snack Allowance

Every employee receives a free box of 12 assorted QQS snacks every month. Yes, this is a real benefit. No, you cannot trade it for cash.
